There is more than one way to move through a life.

Some chapters are built. Some are written. Some are danced. And some begin when you learn to move with the tide.

Chapter I · The person behind the work

Structure in the mind.
Stories in the soul.

A professional life can have rigor without losing wonder.

Reshmi’s world lives at an intersection: thoughtful work, creative instinct, curiosity and a deep appetite for life beyond a job title.

She is a reader who disappears into books, a writer who notices the variables most people walk past, a Bollywood dancer when the music is right, a spiritual seeker, and someone who feels most at home near the ocean.

This is not a résumé rendered as a webpage. It is a glimpse of the person who brings all of those worlds into the way she thinks, works and connects.
